Demo Request FAQ

What to expect when you contact LabGuard HQ.

What should I include in the demo request?

Tell us whether you run a DOT, TPA, probation, court, treatment, or other testing program, plus your approximate donor or participant volume.

Can you help map our current workflow?

Yes. The demo can walk through your current random selection, notification, reporting, and audit-record workflow.

Is LabGuard HQ only for DOT programs?

No. LabGuard HQ supports DOT fleets and TPAs as well as probation departments, courts, treatment programs, and compliance testing organizations.
